Oracle Health Clinical AI Agent Targets Faster Documentation and Improved Reimbursements

New AI capabilities from Oracle Health aims to help clinicians better prepare for patient visits and complete documentation faster.

Something important is quietly changing in healthcare right now — and it's not happening in the operating theatre. AI is taking over the behind-the-scenes work that has long exhausted doctors, nurses, and admin teams: the scheduling, the billing, the referrals, the claims. This matters to anyone who has ever waited weeks for an appointment, chased a referral that fell through the cracks, or watched a loved one's care get held up by paperwork.

Healthcare systems are already using AI to cut through paperwork bottlenecks. Roughly 10% of medical claims are denied on first submission in the US, according to the American Hospital Association. Healthrise's Denials Navigator tool — which includes a large language model — has cut denial rates by an average of 25% for health systems using it. These tools handle the thousands of small decisions that determine whether a hospital gets paid for care it has already delivered.

On the scheduling side, hospitals are seeing real gains. Banner Health partnered with AI platform Qventus to manage inpatient capacity and surgical scheduling, and reported releasing 369 hours of operating room block time every month by predicting cancellations.

Physician concerns remain real. Six in ten doctors surveyed by the American Medical Association said they worry AI will be used to increase claim denials rather than reduce them. Getting this right means pairing smart tools with clear accountability — and that will matter enormously to every patient and family depending on the system to work.

AI in healthcare is moving from promise to plumbing. It is now doing the unglamorous work — chasing claims, answering phones, routing referrals, filling theatre slots — so that doctors and nurses can do what they trained for. The real challenge ahead is building trust and governance alongside the technology.
